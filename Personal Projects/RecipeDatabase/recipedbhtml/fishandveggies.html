Fish & Vegetables

From the kitchen of Eema

  • 1 pkg. flounder fillets
  • 3 potatoes - cubed
  • 1 turnip - cubed
  • 1 onion - sliced
  • 3 carrots - sliced
  • Lay fish on top of vegetables
  • Sprinkle with salt and pepper
  • Pour water in pan to cover vegetables
  • Seal 9x13 pan very tight
  • Bake at 350° for 1 hour
