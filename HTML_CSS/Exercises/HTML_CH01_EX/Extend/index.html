Guideline 1.1 for WCAG states that other than a few exceptions which include controls and inputs, CAPTCHA, and decoration or formatting, all non-text content must have a text alternative.



Guideline 1.2 gives different ways for accessibility in prerecorded audio and video files, including captions, both closed and live, audio and video descriptions, and sign language.



1.3 gives guidelines about how information, structure, and relationships among elements can be determined or are available in text. This means that by just looking at the page a user should be able to understand the correct order of things. You can accomplish this by using a meaningful sequence, Sensory characteristics, and orientation, or by identifying the purpose of the input or user interface.



Guideline 1.4 states that all content must be easily distinguishable - able to been seen and heard clearly, by use of color, contrast, text size and spacing, background audio, hover, and scrolling.
