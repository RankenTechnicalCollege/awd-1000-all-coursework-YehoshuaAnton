Playing the Flute

I've always loved music. When I was in 11th grade, someone brought a keyboard to school and taught me to play one-handed. In 12th grade, someone else brought a guitar, so with nothing else to do, I picked that up also. Towards the end of 12th grade, Covid hit, so I bought a flute and a book and taught myself to play. I'm not very good at it, but I still enjoy playing and listening to music.
