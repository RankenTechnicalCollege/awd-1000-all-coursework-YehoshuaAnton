Playing the Flute

  • My GitHub
  • My LinkedIn

I've always loved music, so when one of my friends brought a keyboard to the dorm in high school, I asked him to teach me how to play. Also because there was nothing else to do. He showed me which keys do what and taught me the notes for a simple song, and I was able to learn more from there. In 12th grade, another one of my friends brought in a guitar, and I was able to pick that up fairly quickly too. Then Covid hit, and stuck at home with nothing else to do, I bought myself a flute and Flute for Dummies, and taught myself to play. I'm still not very good, but I like the sound of the flute and I enjoy playing.
